What is spasms & tear sequelae?

A muscle spasm is sustained tension that causes local pain and limits movement. After a tear, fibrosis, adhesions or strength loss can remain if recovery was incomplete.

What symptoms are linked to spasms & tear sequelae?

They often follow overload, poor training dosage, fatigue, repeated postures or returning too soon. Stress and sleep also shape pain perception.

Without care they can become chronic, flare with effort and create compensations in nearby joints or along the muscle chain.

How is spasms & tear sequelae treated at Kinésica?

At Kinésica we use manual therapy and osteopathy to improve tissue quality, with manipulations when indicated. We integrate muscle-chain work and RPG to reorganize loading and reduce recurrence.

How is spasms & tear sequelae assessed?

Assessment identifies the muscle involved, healing phase and effort tolerance. We progress with mobility, strengthening and a clear return-to-sport or work plan.

  • Progressive weakness, sensory changes, or difficulty walking.
  • Recent trauma, fever, general malaise, or new unusual symptoms.

Frequently asked questions

How can you tell a muscle spasm (contracture) apart from an actual muscle tear?

A spasm builds gradually as a dull, tight ache; a muscle tear happens instantaneously with sharp, stabbing pain (often described as being hit by a stone) and loss of power.

Why does a chronically tight muscle face a higher risk of tearing?

Hypertonic, shortened muscle fibers lack shock absorption and flexibility; during sudden explosive movements, the rigid segment cannot elongate safely and tears.
